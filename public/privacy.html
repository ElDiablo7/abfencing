Privacy notice

This notice explains how AB Fencing uses information submitted through this website.

When you make an enquiry, we may collect your name, telephone number, email address, postcode/area and project details.

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, discuss your project, arrange quotations and provide services. We do not ask for information that is unnecessary for an initial enquiry.

Your enquiry may be handled through the website hosting platform, email provider and any lead-management system configured by AB Fencing. We do not sell enquiry information.

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry, any resulting work and related business records, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Your rights

UK data protection law provides rights relating to personal information, including access and correction in applicable circumstances. To ask about information held by AB Fencing, call 07539 490180.

Updates
